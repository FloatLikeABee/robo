# Instructions
Ask one question at a time. Keep a friendly professional tone.

## Q1 — Full name
- field: name
- collect: text
- required: true
- prompt: What is your full name?

## Q2 — Gender
- field: gender
- collect: mcp_html
- widget: select
- options: [Female, Male, Non-binary, Prefer not to say]
- required: true
- prompt: Please select your gender.

## Q3 — Role
- field: role
- collect: text
- required: true
- prompt: What is your role or job title?

## Q4 — Notes
- field: notes
- collect: text
- required: false
- prompt: Anything else we should know?
